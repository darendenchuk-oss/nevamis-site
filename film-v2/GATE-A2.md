# Gate A2 - rebuilt styleframes, camera & depth, transitions, comprehension

**Visual language: "signal becoming structure."** Deterministic throughout - no
generation, no credits, no TTS. Camera/lighting/dramaturgy vocabulary from
**visual-skills @ `3c55471`** (CC BY 4.0 - **Serge Shima**,
github.com/smixs/visual-skills), applied to deterministic composition.

## What structurally changed

| Critique | Fix |
|---|---|
| Six near-identical dark cards | Only **2 of 6** landscape frames use a card as the primary object (05, 06). 01, 02, 04 are pure signal-in-space; 03 is staggered planes |
| Centred, front-facing | **0 of 6** centred front-facing. Asymmetric framing, `rotateY` 6-17 degrees |
| Sonar as wallpaper | Sonar appears **only where it performs a job** - detecting (01), capturing (02), checking (04), transmitting (05, 08), confirming (06). Different radius, count and opacity each time |
| Information already complete | 03 is caught **mid-transformation**: source phrases underlined mint left, paths in flight, fields arriving on four staggered planes |
| No depth | Three planes per frame: `.bg-far` (blur 2.5px, 50%), `.mid`, `.fg` (blur 3.5px, crossing the frame edge) |
| Two disconnected future cards | 06 is **one directional operating path** - live mint spine, capture node, dashed warm continuation feeding four stages that each visibly inherit a connection |
| Nothing poster-worthy | 04 and 05 built as campaign images; `A2-09-poster-4x5` is a dedicated 1080x1350 crop |
| Vertical was a compressed summary | 08 **natively composed** in three depth zones, copy clear of the top 250px and bottom 340px control strips |

## Motion transition plan - nothing disappears because a slide begins

- **1 to 2** The incoming waveform continues and *reaches* the arc; its bars compress and settle as the mint response enters from the right. Motion relay.
- **2 to 3** The settled bars **stretch and align into the field rows** - a match cut on shape. The caller line stays on screen, receding, becoming frame 3's source transcript.
- **3 to 4** The four field planes converge onto a single path travelling right into the rule boundary. PRICE REQUESTED separates from the group and stops.
- **4 to 5** Everything that passed collapses inward into the lead card, which rotates into foreground as the rejected branch dims to the edge.
- **5 to 6** The delivery signal **continues past** the message preview; camera pulls back and the path is revealed as one section of the operating spine.
- **6 to 7** The spine contracts along its own length into the arc, and the capture node becomes the mint dot of the real mark.

Easing `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; exits `cubic-bezier(.7,0,.84,0)`.
