## Why 48 and not 62

62 spent eleven seconds establishing before the first idea landed. At 48 the
hook arrives at 0:08 and every beat carries one meaning. Cuts made: the second
drift-wide is folded into the first, the lattice extension is shortened to a
single move, and the silhouette beat is trimmed to a held breath. Nothing that
carries a capability was removed.

## Narrator — recommendation

Searched the professional shared library (300 unique English professional
voices) and ranked on the evidence the directive asks for: 1-year usage, clone
count, featured status, then filtered to middle-aged North American and scored
for restraint.

**Recommended — Jon, "Warm & Grounded"**
`voice_id: MFZUKuGQUsGJPQjTS4wC`
*"A warm, trustworthy American voice with a mature and grounded tone. Ideal for
narration and commercials."* · 6.85B characters generated in 1y — the highest
of any candidate · 797,895 clones · descriptor **calm**.

Chosen because the film's authority has to come from restraint. Jon reads as
someone stating facts he is sure of, which is what a 71-word script needs.

## Music and sound design

| Section | Music | Sound design |
|---|---|---|
| C01–C02 | **None.** Room tone only — a vast, cold space | One soft impact as the first object passes |
| C03 | A single low sub-drop as objects fall | The dimming has a sound: a short descending absence |
| C04 | **Pulse enters** — 62 bpm, one note, felt more than heard | **Motif cue #1**: two-note fall, low, on the lattice resolving |
| C05–C06 | Pulse gains a floor; a second harmonic | Impact as the call lands, then quiet |
| C07 | Pulse detail — glass articulations on the beat | Each pane opening has its own soft transient |
| C08 | Held, no growth — tension through stillness | **Motif cue #2**, quieter, as the rule holds the quote |
| C09 | First warmth in the harmony | **Motif cue #3** on `CONFIRMED`, the only bright cue in the film |
| C10 | Opens out — the pulse becomes a field | Trails have doppler; scale is audible |
| C11 | **Music resolves and stops** | Room tone alone. The silence is the payoff |
| C12 | Silence | Wordmark lands on a single low tail, then nothing |

**The motif** is NEVAMIS's sonic signature: a two-note descending figure, ~180ms,
low-mid, more felt than heard. Three appearances, never more. It becomes the
website interaction cue and the ad ident.

**No** whoosh transitions, no riser into the logo, no corporate ukulele, no EDM
drop. Music licensed or original only.

## Wordmark animation — real typography

Built from the actual font file, rendered deterministically. **No AI lettering
anywhere.**

- **Face:** Bricolage Grotesque 700, self-hosted variable font already in the
  repo (`assets/fonts/`), tracking +0.18em.
- **Build:** letterforms → SVG paths via the real font → per-frame PNG sequence
  → ffmpeg. Type is therefore identical to the website's type, because it *is*
  the website's type.
- **Animation, 3.5s:**
  - `0.0–0.6s` black holds; a single mint point of light remains from C09
  - `0.6–1.4s` **N E V A M I S** resolve left to right, 90ms apart, each rising
    4px and settling with a 0.35s ease — the same `--e-out` curve the website
    uses
  - `1.4–1.8s` tracking tightens from +0.24em to +0.18em as the mint point fades
  - `1.8–2.2s` hold
  - `2.2–2.8s` the line fades up beneath at 80% white
  - `2.8–3.5s` hold, then cut to black on the sound tail
- **Rule:** the wordmark never rotates, never has a symbol locked to it, never
  gets a flare, and is never followed by "AI".
