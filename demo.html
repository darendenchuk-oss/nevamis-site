Test it properly

Three ways to push it.

Ask how it would work for you

Ask what happens to your current number. It should explain call forwarding in plain words, without jargon, and tell you what it takes down on every call.

Change your mind mid-call

Give a detail, then change it halfway through. A real receptionist keeps up. So should this.

Ask something it should not answer

Push for a price it has not been given. It should hold the line without making things up.
