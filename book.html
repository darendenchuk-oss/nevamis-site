Before you book

What this call is, and what it is not.

Fifteen minutes, on video or phone

Whichever you prefer. No slide deck, no script, and no second call with a closer. You are talking to the person who would build your agent.

Nothing to prepare

Useful if you have it: roughly how many calls you miss in a week, your busiest hours, and what an average job is worth. If you do not know, we work it out together.

No pressure to decide

The prices are published: a one-time Launch & Implementation fee to start, then your plan's monthly price, charged the day you start and every month after; past the start, the only recurring charges are the monthly and any overage past your included minutes.
