Privacy

Last updated: July 2026. This page describes what this website collects. It is written to be read, not to hide things.

Who we are

This service is operated by Nevamis AI Inc., a federal corporation based in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. We handle personal information under Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where it applies. This page is written to be read, not to hide things, and it is not legal advice.

What this website collects

This website does not use advertising trackers and does not set non-essential cookies. It stores two small things in your browser, and neither ever leaves your device: your motion on/off choice, and a short-lived note that you tapped a call button, so that when you come back to the tab we can offer the obvious next step once instead of asking again. Fonts are self-hosted on nevamis.ca, so viewing this site sends no requests to Google or any other font provider. We count page views and button clicks first-party on our own systems to understand what works: each count records only the event name, the page path, the referring site’s hostname, and campaign tags. No cookies, no IP addresses, and no identifiers are stored, and the counts never leave Nevamis.

When you call the demo line

Calls to (587) 413-0035 are handled by an AI voice agent running on third-party telephony and voice platforms. Call audio, transcripts, and any details you share (such as your name or callback number) are processed to run the conversation and to follow up with you if you ask us to. Do not share sensitive personal information on the demo line.

When you book a call

Bookings are handled by our scheduling provider (Cal.com). The information you enter there (name, email, and anything you add) is used to run the meeting and follow up. Their own privacy terms apply to their processing.

When you email us

Email to Sales@nevamis.ca is used to reply to you. We do not sell contact information.

Where your data is processed

To run the demo, scheduling, voice, and email, we use trusted service providers that may store and process personal information in the United States and other countries. This means your information may be handled outside Canada and may be subject to the laws of those countries. We use providers that offer commercially reasonable safeguards, and we share only what is needed to deliver the service.

How long we keep it

Website preferences stay on your device only. Contact and scheduling details are kept while we are in touch and for a reasonable follow-up period, then removed. Demo-line audio and transcripts are used to run and improve the demo; we are reducing retention with our voice provider to a fixed maximum, and in the meantime we delete this data on request. We keep only what we need for the purposes described here.

Your rights: see, correct, or delete your information

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email Sales@nevamis.ca and we will respond within 30 days. We will delete information from the systems we control and ask our providers to delete their copies, except where we are required to keep something by law.

For businesses using Nevamis

Businesses deploying a Nevamis agent on their own phone line are the party responsible for the caller notices, recording disclosure, and consent appropriate to their jurisdiction. We provide the configuration and guidance; the business decides its recording settings and gives callers notice. A written agreement sets out who is responsible for what.

Privacy contact

Nevamis AI Inc. has a designated person accountable for privacy. Reach them at Sales@nevamis.ca, Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. If you have a concern we cannot resolve, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
