Straight answers

Questions owners actually ask.

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

The moment anyone asks, yes, plainly and without hedging. It does not open the call by announcing itself, any more than a receptionist opens by explaining who employs them: it greets the caller in your business's name and gets on with the job. Dial the demo line and ask it directly.

Will it sound robotic?

Call (587) 413-0035 and judge. It speaks in a natural voice, handles interruptions, and keeps answers short the way a good receptionist does.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. Your number stays exactly as it is. Call forwarding sends calls to Nevamis always, after hours, or only when you miss the call. You can switch it off from your own phone at any time.

Can it answer only after hours or when we miss a call?

Yes. That is exactly what conditional forwarding does: you answer when you can, Nevamis catches everything else instead of voicemail.

Can it book directly into my calendar?

Not yet, and we will not pretend otherwise. The assistant takes the job, the address and the time the caller wants, then texts it to you so you can confirm the slot. Direct calendar booking is on the roadmap and is not live on any line today.

What happens when a caller has an unusual question?

The agent only commits to what your rules allow. For anything outside them it takes a message and flags the summary, in the way we configure escalation with you.

What happens on an urgent call?

NEVAMIS captures the urgent details and alerts your team. We define together what counts as urgent for your business, and a call that meets it is flagged that way the moment it ends. Live transfer to a person is not part of the service.

Can you cover more than one location, or more than one language?

Not today. A plan covers one business phone line, and multi-location or multi-department routing is not something we can provision, so we do not sell it. If that is what you need, say so on the strategy call and we will tell you honestly whether to wait for us or use somebody else.

How long does setup take?

Days, not months. Discovery, build, written call scenarios run at the agent before a number is pointed at it, then your forwarding proven with a real call.

Can I review calls and summaries?

Yes. Every call produces a summary, and call review is part of how we tune your agent over time.

What does it cost?

Two published numbers, joined by one rule. The AI Front Desk, the start most businesses make and the recommended plan, is C$1,500 Launch & Implementation to start, then C$1,000 a month, with no performance fee. The Works, the whole engine with every sellable automation included, is C$3,000 Launch & Implementation to start, then C$2,100 a month, also with no performance fee. Automation add-ons join the Front Desk one at a time: Missed-Call Recovery C$350 a month, Quote-Chase Engine C$500, Get-Paid Autopilot C$500, Review Engine C$300. Canadian dollars plus GST/HST. There is a three-month start on the Front Desk (six with add-ons or The Works): the build takes days and your line is answered inside the first week, the results show across the months after that, and after the minimum it is month to month you control from your portal. The full ladder, including the invitation-based Performance Partnership, is on the pricing page, and your exact fit is confirmed on a short strategy call.

What does the Launch & Implementation fee cover?

The build, priced in the open instead of hidden in the monthly. Before you go live your agent is configured around your hours, services, prices, service area and rules, your integrations and automations are connected, a baseline of your business is taken, and the system is run against written call scenarios before a phone number is pointed at it. That is real work, it happens once, and the one-time fee is charged once, at the start, beside your first month, never again.

Can I try it before I commit?

There is no trial, paid or free, and the seven-day paid pilot Nevamis used to sell is retired. What replaced it is a start with the length published in advance: three months on the AI Front Desk, six months when any add-on or The Works is included. After that it continues month to month on 30 days notice and you cancel it yourself from the portal. The build takes days, so by the end of the first week you are judging your own call recordings rather than a promise. More on the how you start page.

How is customer information handled?

Call details are used to run your service: the lead record, your summaries, and the recording in your portal. See the privacy page for what this website itself collects. Businesses remain responsible for notices appropriate to their own jurisdiction and call-recording requirements.

What if it is not a fit?

Past your minimum term you cancel it yourself from the portal, on 30 days notice, and service runs to the end of the period you already paid for. There is no cancellation fee and nobody to talk into letting you go. The term is published before you buy rather than discovered in the agreement: three months on the AI Front Desk, six months when any add-on or The Works is included.
