What you pay, and when.

The Works, the three Front Desk sizes and every add-on are priced as a monthly alone: the discovery call, configuration, testing and go-live are part of it. Buy now charges your plan's first month the day you start, and the same date every month after. The only other recurring charges are the monthly of any add-on you choose, overage past your included minutes and, on the Performance Partnership, offered by invitation, the agreed share of attributable collected revenue set in your agreement.

The Performance Partnership is the one published plan with a Launch & Implementation fee: C$5,000 Launch & Implementation to start. Offered by invitation and approval. Enterprise is quoted per client, its Launch & Implementation starting at C$5,000.

There is no minimum term. Every plan and every add-on, bought on its own or added later, is month to month from the first month: cancel any time from your own portal, service running to the end of the month you already paid for, and your price locked for 12 months from signing.

How usage works

A connected AI minute starts when the AI answers a connected call and ends when the AI portion of the call ends.

  • Failed calls that never connect are not counted.
  • Wrong numbers or spam that reach the AI consume usage, because the system handled them.
  • A text or email alert after you pass 50%, 75%, 90% or 100% of your included minutes, with your running total on your portal's billing page.
  • Past your included minutes, calls keep being answered and each extra minute is billed at your plan's per-minute rate, shown on its card above.
  • Once you pass all of your included minutes, the email alert names your plan's rate for each extra minute and, on a Front Desk size with a larger one above it, what the next size includes and costs. Your plan never changes unless you change it.
