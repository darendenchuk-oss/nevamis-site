The first Revenue Engine build

This is development work, not a product you can buy.

The front desk is a live product on a published monthly price. The Revenue Engine is not: it is in development, and the first builds are applied for rather than bought. Applying is a conversation about whether your business is a sensible place to build it first.

  • Scoped small on purpose: one location, one service line and one advertising channel, with each lead from it followed to a paid job.
  • You own and fund your own advertising account. Advertising, telephony, messaging, and data are disclosed pass-through costs you pay directly.
  • There is no published Revenue Engine price and no standing offer. Commercial terms are scoped and confirmed in writing before anything starts, or there is no engagement.
  • No minimum number of leads, appointments, or revenue is guaranteed, and none is implied by anything on this page.
  • Founder-led, and accepted on fit and onboarding capacity rather than on order of arrival.

Nothing here is a price list. Your front desk plan, if you have one, is unaffected by any of this: it stays its own published plan, with a monthly you can cancel yourself from the portal at any time, because there is no minimum term and no notice period on anything.

Questions

Straight answers.

Can I buy the Revenue Engine today?

Not yet. It is in private development, applications to be part of the first builds are open, with no clients onboarded yet. Everything live today, from the front desk to quote recovery, is on the pricing page with its price, and Lead Generation is offered by invitation.

How is this different from the front desk?

The front desk answers your phone and is live today on a published price. The Revenue Engine is unreleased development work on attribution: tying each lead to the marketing that produced it and following it to a paid job, using your own advertising account. Nothing about it is sold or priced yet.

Do you guarantee more revenue?

No. You control capacity, pricing, service delivery, and close rate. We measure and report the results honestly. The calculator above is an illustration, not a promise.

Who owns the advertising account?

It would stay yours. Nothing about this runs today. If it is built for your business, you keep the advertising account and fund it directly, and nothing is spent or changed on it without your approval.

What does it cost?

Nothing is priced. There is no Revenue Engine price, and none will be published until it is built and measured. Everything live today has its price on the pricing page.
