How the customer journey works

Seven steps, one connected system.

Each step reuses the same calls, forms, calendars, and customer history, so the parts get more valuable together instead of starting from zero.

  1. Capture

    A call, form, or quote request becomes a tracked lead with its source recorded.

  2. Respond

    Approved follow-up begins within seconds instead of waiting for someone free.

  3. Qualify

    Your rules decide who is a real opportunity, in your service area, for a service you offer.

  4. Book or route

    Real calendar availability, or a transfer to a person when that is the right call.

  5. Follow up

    No-shows, idle quotes, and slow replies get a short approved sequence that stops on any reply.

  6. Track the job

    Quote, job, invoice, and collected payment are recorded against the lead that started it.

  7. Prove the revenue

    You see which source produced booked and collected revenue, with the evidence behind every number.

The 30-day Revenue Engine pilot

A separate pilot from the 7-day Front Desk pilot.

The AI Front Desk pilot is seven live days for C$150, credited toward your first month if you continue. The Revenue Engine pilot is a different, larger engagement. It is limited to a small number of pilot spots and is applied for, not signed up for on the spot.

  • Thirty days, one location, one service line, one lead channel, one landing page, one tracked phone flow, one calendar, one CRM pipeline, one approved follow-up sequence.
  • You own and fund your own advertising account. Advertising, telephony, messaging, and data are disclosed pass-through costs you pay directly.
  • Nevamis management and performance fees are waived during the pilot. A configurable activation fee protects founder onboarding time and can be credited to your first paid month if you continue.
  • Leads created during the pilot are permanently pilot-exempt and never create a later performance fee. No minimum number of leads, appointments, or revenue is guaranteed.
  • Pilots are founder-led and accepted based on fit and onboarding capacity.

Thirty days of Revenue Engine management free. Client-funded advertising, activation, and usage costs may apply. Exact terms are confirmed in writing before anything starts.

Questions

Straight answers.

Can I buy the Revenue Engine today?

Not yet. It is in private development, and pilot applications are open with no clients onboarded yet. You can apply to be considered. The AI Front Desk, on the other hand, is available now.

How is this different from the 7-day Front Desk pilot?

The Front Desk pilot proves the phone agent on your line in seven free days. The Revenue Engine pilot is a separate 30-day engagement that adds lead acquisition, follow-up, and revenue attribution, and it involves your own advertising account.

Do you guarantee more revenue?

No. You control capacity, pricing, service delivery, and close rate. We measure and report the results honestly. The calculator above is an illustration, not a promise.

Who owns the advertising account?

You do. You fund it directly. Nevamis receives authorized management access and never raises your budget without your approval.

What does it cost?

Pricing is scoped to your setup and confirmed in writing. There is no published Revenue Engine price yet; it is set with real pilot data. See the AI Front Desk pricing page for current published plans.
