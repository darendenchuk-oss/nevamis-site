The signal path

How a missed call becomes a booked job.

Every call moves through the same precise sequence. You approve the rules once. Nevamis runs them on every ring.

  1. Call comes in

    Your number forwards to Nevamis when you choose: always, after hours, or when you can't pick up.

  2. Answered in your tone

    It answers in your business's voice and style, within seconds.

  3. Caller qualified

    It asks the questions you approved: job type, urgency, location, timing.

  4. Job taken down

    It captures the job, the address, and the time they want, following the rules you approved.

  5. Caller told what happens next

    Nobody is left guessing: the caller is told plainly that someone will confirm the exact time, and never that it is already booked.

  6. You get the summary

    Name, number, need, and outcome land in your pocket after every call.

Straight answers

Questions owners actually ask.

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

The moment anyone asks, yes, plainly and without hedging. It does not open the call by announcing itself, any more than a receptionist opens by explaining who employs them: it greets the caller in your business's name and gets on with the job. Dial the demo line and ask it directly.

Will it sound robotic?

Call (587) 413-0035 and judge. It speaks in a natural voice, handles interruptions, and keeps answers short the way a good receptionist does.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. Your number stays exactly as it is. Call forwarding sends calls to Nevamis always, after hours, or only when you miss the call. You can switch it off from your own phone at any time.

Can it answer only after hours or when we miss a call?

Yes. That is exactly what conditional forwarding does: you answer when you can, Nevamis catches everything else instead of voicemail.

Can it book directly into my calendar?

Not yet, and we will not pretend otherwise. The assistant takes the job, the address and the time the caller wants, then texts it to you so you can confirm the slot. Direct calendar booking is on the roadmap and is not live on any line today.

What happens when a caller has an unusual question?

The agent only commits to what your rules allow. For anything outside them it takes a message, flags the summary, or transfers, depending on how we configure escalation with you.

Can it transfer urgent calls?

Yes, live transfer to a number you choose is a configurable rule. We define together what counts as urgent for your business.

Can you cover more than one location, or more than one language?

Not today. A plan covers one business phone line, and multi-location or multi-department routing is not something we can provision, so we do not sell it. If that is what you need, say so on the strategy call and we will tell you honestly whether to wait for us or use somebody else.

How long does setup take?

Days, not months. Discovery, build, written call scenarios run at the agent before a number is pointed at it, then your forwarding proven with a real call.

Can I review calls and summaries?

Yes. Every call produces a summary, and call review is part of how we tune your agent over time.

What does it cost?

One price a month, charged the day you start and every month after: C$250 on Core, C$500 on Growth, C$1,000 on Pro. There is no setup fee, no activation fee and no onboarding charge, so your first bill is the same as your second. Canadian dollars plus GST/HST, month to month, cancel anytime. What each plan includes is on the pricing page, and your exact fit is confirmed on a short strategy call.

Why is there nothing to pay up front?

Building it is real work and it happens before you go live: your agent is configured around your hours, services, prices, service area and rules, then run against written call scenarios before a phone number is pointed at it. We price that inside the monthly fee rather than beside it, because a second number on day one is the thing every buyer has to stop and work out. What you avoid is a cheaper monthly price attached to a template that does not know your business.

Can I try it before I commit?

There is no trial, paid or free, and the seven-day paid pilot Nevamis used to sell is retired. What replaced it is a shorter commitment than any trial: you start on a plan, you are charged monthly, and you can cancel from your portal on any day, with service running to the end of the month you paid for. You judge it from your own call recordings in the portal rather than from a promise. More on the how you start page.

How is customer information handled?

Call details are used to run your service: the lead record, your summaries, and the recording in your portal. See the privacy page for what this website itself collects. Businesses remain responsible for notices appropriate to their own jurisdiction and call-recording requirements.

What if it is not a fit?

You cancel it yourself from the portal, and service runs to the end of the month you already paid for. There is no term to serve out, no cancellation fee, and nobody to talk into letting you go. The most it can cost you to find out is one month.
