Illustrative workflow · fictional company

One after-hours call, start to finish.

A local HVAC company gets a no-cooling call at 9 PM. Here is the connected journey, with each step marked live or planned.

  1. The front desk answers in seconds, any hour. (Live today.)
  2. Service area and urgency are checked against the company's approved rules. (Live today.)
  3. The job and the time they want are taken down, or the call is flagged urgent and your team is alerted with the details. (Live today.)
  4. The call becomes a tracked lead with its source, its status and the call summary. (Live today.)
  5. The caller is told plainly that the office will confirm the exact time. (Live today.)
  6. The owner gets the summary. (Live today.)
  7. The open estimate gets approved follow-up the day it goes quiet, four days on and eleven days on. (Live today.)
  8. The finished job gets one review request by text, released by a person. (Live today.)
  9. A maintenance reminder becomes eligible next season. (Being researched.)
  10. Collected revenue is linked back to that original 9 PM call. (In development.)

Straight answers

Roadmap questions.

Are these services available now?

Some are. Anything marked AVAILABLE NOW on this page is live today and described exactly as narrowly as it works. Lead Generation is offered by invitation, and a short call is where it starts. Everything else is planned or being researched, and may change before it is ready.

Do I have to start with the front desk?

No. The front desk works on its own, and so does each module on the pricing page. Lead Generation is by invitation, and a short call is where it starts.

Can I buy one service without the whole stack?

Yes, today. Missed-Call Recovery, the Quote-Chase Engine, Get-Paid Autopilot and the Review Engine are each sold on their own, and The Works bundles them with the AI Front Desk. Each one's price is on the pricing page.

Will these work with my current software?

Not through an integration. There are none today: no CRM, job-management or accounting connection. The front desk works on your existing number through call forwarding, every call becomes a lead you can read in your portal and export as a CSV, and the summary comes to you by text and email.

Will anything be sent or changed without your approval?

No. Sensitive actions like pricing, complaints, and unusual replies are designed to require human approval, and message templates are approved by you first.

How will my customer information be protected?

Data is used only to run the service you configure, with retention and access rules agreed before launch. See our privacy page for how this website itself behaves.

Will this replace my employees?

The goal is to remove repetitive phone and office work, not people. Humans stay in charge of judgment calls, pricing, and anything unusual.

Can I be one of the first to use something on this page?

Tell us on the form which service would help. When something here is ready, it gets a published price before anyone signs up for it, and we will tell you. Lead Generation is by invitation, and a short call is where it starts.

Does submitting interest lock me into anything?

No. It is feedback that shapes what we build, nothing more.

How will future pricing work?

Nothing planned on this page has a price yet. Each one gets a published price before it launches, the same way the live services are priced today. Lead Generation is different: it is by invitation and arranged on a call.

Can I suggest a different workflow?

Yes, please do. The form has a free-text field for exactly that, and the best suggestions shape the roadmap.

How will Nevamis know a service is working?

Each service has a defined success metric before it launches, like response time for follow-up or recovered revenue for quotes, measured against your baseline.
