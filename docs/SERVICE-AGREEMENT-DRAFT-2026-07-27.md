## Part A: the 7-day live pilot (free)

1. What it is: Nevamis configures and runs an AI receptionist on one phone
   line, one call flow, and one calendar for your business for 7 days.
2. What it costs: nothing. No card is collected and nothing is charged when
   the pilot ends. Silence never becomes a subscription.
3. Caps: up to 60 connected AI minutes or 30 calls, whichever comes first,
   and one configuration revision during the week. At 75% of the cap we
   tell you; at 100% the line falls back to your voicemail or chosen number.
4. The clock: day one starts at go-live (your forwarding active and a test
   call passed), not at signup. Downtime caused by Nevamis or a provider
   pauses the clock; we add the lost time back.
5. Day eight: we review the call log together and you decide: subscribe, or
   we switch forwarding back and delete your configuration on request.
6. Your part: set the carrier forwarding we give you steps for, tell your
   team the AI is answering, and give accurate business information.
7. Data: calls are recorded and transcribed to run the service and produce
   your daily report. Handling and deletion follow the privacy policy.

## Part B: paid service (after the pilot)

1. Plan and price: the plan, monthly fee, setup fee, included minutes, and
   overage rate are the ones on nevamis.ca/pricing at signing, written into
   the order summary below. Prices are CAD plus GST/HST.
2. Billing: monthly in advance by card through Stripe. Overage is billed as
   described on the pricing page, with usage alerts before it applies.
3. Term: month to month. Cancel any time by email before renewal; service
   runs to the end of the paid period. No cancellation fees.
4. Price changes: at least 30 days written notice; the new price applies
   from your next renewal after the notice period.
5. What Nevamis does: configure, test, run, monitor, and tune the agent;
   deliver call summaries and reports; provide the escalation path; keep
   your business rules under version control with rollback.
6. What you do: keep forwarding active (or tell us to pause), keep business
   facts current, handle the caller notices and recording consent that
   apply to your business, and respond to escalations that need you.
7. Third parties: the service runs on telephony, voice, scheduling, and
   payment providers. Their outages are not a breach by Nevamis; we commit
   to commercially reasonable efforts and honest status communication.
8. Liability: neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses.
   Nevamis's total liability in any 12 months is capped at the fees you
   paid in the 3 months before the event. Nothing limits what the law does
   not allow to be limited.
9. Suspension: we may suspend service used unlawfully or abusively, with
   notice and reasons.
10. Ending: on termination we return or delete your business data on
    request, help you restore your original call routing, and stop billing.
11. Law: Alberta law, Edmonton venue, good-faith resolution first.
12. Whole agreement: this agreement plus the referenced pricing page and
    privacy policy are the entire deal; it wins over the website terms
    where they differ.
